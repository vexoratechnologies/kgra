MY KGRA

Official Mobile Application • Kerala Government Radiographers' Association

App Overview
What is My KGRA?

My KGRA is the official mobile application for government hospital radiographers across Kerala. It is a simple, secure digital platform that gives members instant access to official association news, Government Orders, educational webinars, and digital membership services directly from their smartphones.

Who is it for?
  • Government Hospital Radiographers: Radiographers and technologists working in Medical Colleges and Hospitals (DHS & DME).
  • Association Leaders: State Committee, Executive Council, and Zonal committee representatives.
  • New Recruits & Retired Members: Fresh joinees seeking guidance and senior retired members staying connected.
What Members Can Do (Key Features)

👥 State Committee Leadership Directory

View official State Committee leaders in ranked hierarchy (President, General Secretary, Treasurer, etc.) with direct phone contacts and live search.

📍 Executive & Zonal Committees

Access designated Executive Council delegates and regional zonal committees across all 14 districts in Kerala.

📢 Instant Announcements

Real-time push alerts for official association news, press releases, and circulars.

📄 Government Orders (G.O.) & Circulars

Read and download official Health Department orders, service guidelines, and meeting minutes with built-in PDF viewing.

💳 Easy Online Payments

Pay annual membership dues and welfare funds quickly and securely via UPI, cards, or net banking.

🎓 Webinars & Training (CME)

Watch medical imaging tutorials, clinical lectures, and live CME webinars directly within the app.

🪪 Digital Membership Profile

Instant digital verification of your active membership status, professional designation, and assigned zone.
